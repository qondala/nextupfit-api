### ProgramFreetool
Freetools are Activities that do not belong to any program.
Freetools are public and free/purchasable.
Freetools do not impose any accessibility or visibility constraints.
Freetools do not impose a flow to the user. Which means that once user starts a freetool activity,
he can consume sessions and workouts in any order.
